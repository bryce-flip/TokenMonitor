<objective>
Harden the tracer's fetch path against provider failure modes: discriminate the three -32005 shapes (result cap vs query timeout vs throttle), halve sub-ranges deterministically under result caps with a fail-closed floor, keep bounded backoff for throttles, close the Phase 1 deferred credential-redaction gap in wrapped errors, and probe finalized-tag support with a loud fallback (research A1).

Purpose: SYNC-02 — retries transient RPC failures and rate limits without skipping a range or advancing the checkpoint. Implements D-05 (fixed window, deterministic halving down to a floor, then fail) and the AR-01 scheduled redaction item from 01-SECURITY.md.
Output: FetchLogsResilient on the rpc client, the sync loop switched onto it, tag-probe fallback at startup, and the discrimination/redaction test proofs.
</objective>
